✦ We are EduVenture

A community for New York's pre-service and new teachers 纽约教师成长社群 · 探索 · 支持 · 成长

We're a community built by and for pre-service teachers in New York. We started this space with a few simple goals in mind:

  • Sharing practical tips for people working toward becoming teachers — job hunting, classroom activity design, demo lessons, and more
  • Continuing to support each other with the stress and anxiety that comes after you've started the job
  • Creating a genuine safe space to vent and share, without judgment
  • Encouraging everyone to speak openly and honestly

Where the name comes from

We named this community EduVenture to capture something we all share: we're all explorers — adventurers — in the field of education. Choosing education, and staying on this path, is a lot like being an adventurer. Education keeps changing as fast as the world around it does, and that means we have to stay ready to push through whatever gets in the way.

我们给这个小社区取名 EduVenture，意在表达我们都是教育领域的探索者、冒险者。

选择教育，并在教育这条道路一直走下去的我们，就像是冒险家，教育随着社会发展也日新月异，我们也需要随时做好披荆斩棘的准备！
